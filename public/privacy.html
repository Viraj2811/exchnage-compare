Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page is a plain-language template. Have a qualified lawyer review it for your country before you launch.

The short version

No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email, bank details or wallet address, and you should never type them into this tool.

What is stored on your device

Your last choices (coin, currency, buy or sell, and any fee changes) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are ready next time. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

How prices are loaded

Your browser asks our server for prices. Our server reads public order-book data from exchange websites. Exchanges do not receive your personal details, only a request from our server. Our server keeps your IP address in memory for up to one minute to limit abuse and does not store it afterwards. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Analytics

If analytics is switched on, we use Plausible, a privacy-focused tool that does not use cookies or collect personal data. It counts visits and clicks on exchange links.

Exchange links

The Trade buttons lead to exchange websites. Some may be affiliate links, which are marked as sponsored. When you click one you leave this site and that exchange's own privacy policy applies.

Children

This tool is meant for adults and is not aimed at anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this page and will change the date above when we do. Questions: contact@YOUR-DOMAIN.com
